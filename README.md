# Night Productivity

A Minecraft Paper plugin that turns the late night experience into a psychological survival hazard.

You can always stay awake at night.. but do you dare to ? Will the hallucinations get to you ?

Your vision *will* get blurry, you *will* feel dizzy, move heavier, items will start *slipping* from your hands, and... you'll start seeing things..

Maybe the PTSD wasn't cured after all.

[Watch the gameplay demo](https://youtu.be/Yt21-PsNMZc)

## How it works

Instead of just slapping a debuff of night owl players, **Night Productivity** tracks players who push past the night without having rest
and gradually, they'll start hallucinating things:

- Blindness will kick in for a few seconds, that's your body saying you need to rest
- You will feel dizzy, as if the world is spinning, your brain is giving out
- Your strength will be drained out, items will start slipping through your grip!
- Dark memories will be relived, back that night.. in the cave.. where you lost all your items to a sneaky, tall explosive grass... *PTSD in Minecraft is real..*

## Requirements 
- **Server:** Paper 1.21.x (1.21.11 Recommended)
- **Dependency:** [PacketEvents](https://modrinth.com/plugin/packetevents)

## Installation
1. Install **PacketEvents** on your Paper server by dragging it into the `plugins` folder.
2. Download the latest `.jar` from [Releases](https://github.com/molham-hq/night-prod/releases/tag/release) and drop it in your `plugins` folder.
3. Start the server and configure your settings in `config.yml`.
